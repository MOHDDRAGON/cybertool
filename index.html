Security Utilities

Hash Generator

Generate MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256, and SHA-512 digests of arbitrary text, live, in your browser.

MD5 and SHA-1 are shown for legacy/compatibility reference only — both are cryptographically broken and unsuitable for password storage or integrity guarantees. Prefer SHA-256 or SHA-512.

Password Strength Analyzer

Heuristic entropy estimate plus pattern checks. Nothing you type here is stored or sent anywhere — refresh the page and it's gone.
